2. Privacy at a glance

This website is a purely static information page — no cookies, no tracking, no analytics tools, no contact forms, no external fonts. Kallilex, the app itself, sends no telemetry.

3. Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This website is hosted by GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. GitHub collects technically necessary server logs when the page is accessed (including your IP address) for providing and securing the website. The legal basis is Article 6 (1) lit. f GDPR (legitimate interest in secure and stable provision of the website). Data transfers to the USA occur; GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Further information: GitHub Privacy Statement .

4. Release information (GitHub API)

When the homepage loads, the browser queries the public GitHub API (api.github.com) for the latest version of Kallilex; your IP address is transmitted to GitHub. The legal basis is Article 6 (1) lit. f GDPR. No personal data is stored during this process.

5. Email contact

When contacting via email, the information provided is processed to handle your inquiry (Article 6 (1) lit. b or f GDPR) and is deleted once it is no longer required for the purpose.

6. SSL/TLS encryption

This site uses SSL/TLS encryption (HTTPS) for security reasons.

7. iOS App (Kallilex)

The iOS app Kallilex collects no data. Text that you rewrite with the app is sent only to the AI provider you configure yourself — not to the app developer.

  • API keys are stored only in your iPhone's Keychain, are not synced to iCloud, and are sent only in the requests to the provider you chose.
  • Text is sent directly from your device to the provider you configure; that provider's privacy policy applies to its processing. With the "Apple Intelligence (on device)" provider, text never leaves your device.
  • There is no telemetry, no tracking, and no analytics tools.
  • The app is open source (Apache-2.0 license).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Article 77 GDPR).
